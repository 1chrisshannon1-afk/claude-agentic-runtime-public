# Hiring KB — index

Knowledge base for `hiring-specialist`: helping a small US employer hire staff (employees and individual contract workers)
(crew, office staff, healthcare staff, engineers, salespeople). Company-agnostic — each company's
roles, pay bands, locations and boards live in its own hiring dossier. Every claim follows
`../marketing_kb/shared/citation-style.md`. Employment law varies by state and changes often:
these files are information, not legal advice; an employment attorney decides.
