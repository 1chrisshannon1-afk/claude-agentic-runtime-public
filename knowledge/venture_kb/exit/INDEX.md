# Exit knowledge base — index

**What this file answers.** Where to find guidance on selling a company — two tracks, four files.
The **software track** covers who buys a small software company, how it's valued, how the sale
process runs, and how the deal itself gets structured and diligenced. The **local service business
track** covers valuing and selling a small owner-operated home-services/trade business (SDE
multiples, SBA 7(a) buyer financing, contractor license/insurance transfer, business brokers, and
exclusive-listing mechanics). Read the reading order below for both tracks before jumping into any
one file.

## Files

- **[selling-a-small-software-company.md](selling-a-small-software-company.md)** — buyer types
  (strategic acquirers, PE/holding companies, search funds, individual marketplace buyers, brokers)
  and what each pays for; valuing pre-revenue/tiny-revenue software (asset sale, acqui-hire,
  replacement cost) versus revenue-generating software (sourced ARR-band multiples); the sale
  process step by step (teaser → NDA → CIM → management calls → LOI → exclusivity → diligence →
  purchase agreement → close) and timelines; reaching buyers via corporate development outreach;
  creating competitive tension between multiple interested parties; and what kills deals.

- **[deal-structure-and-diligence.md](deal-structure-and-diligence.md)** — asset sale vs. stock sale
  (tax treatment via the IRS residual method and Form 8594, liability differences); earnouts (and the
  SBA's flat prohibition on seller earnouts in an SBA-financed deal), escrows/holdbacks, reps and
  warranties, non-competes (including the actual court-vacatur-then-FTC-accession-then-CFR-removal
  sequence and current federal/state enforceability status), transition services agreements, and
  seller notes; what's actually binding in a Letter of Intent versus not, and exclusivity/no-shop
  mechanics; the data-room index for a small SaaS company (IP assignment chain, open-source licenses,
  customer contracts, security posture, HIPAA business-associate exposure for health-data products,
  financials and tax); common diligence red flags and how to fix each before going to market; and
  M&A advisor/banker fee structures (Lehman and Double Lehman formulas) including when a deal is too
  small to justify one.

- **[selling-a-local-service-business.md](selling-a-local-service-business.md)** — how a small
  owner-operated home-services business is valued (SDE/EBITDA multiples by deal-size band), what
  raises and lowers the multiple (owner dependence, license/insurance status, recurring revenue,
  customer concentration, related-party revenue), SBA 7(a) buyer-financing mechanics (equity
  injection; seller-note standby and its 36-month refinancing-eligibility rule; the personal-guarantee
  rule split at 20% retained ownership; the seller's post-closing role capped at a 24-month
  consultant arrangement, never employment), contractor license and insurance transfer issues on a
  sale, what a buyer diligences, and how a rebuilt marketing/lead engine raises price. 47 claim ids
  [KB-VSALE-001]–[KB-VSALE-047] (plus lettered sub-ids added for corrections).

- **[business-brokers-and-exclusives.md](business-brokers-and-exclusives.md)** — how exclusive
  right-to-sell broker listings work (commission ranges and structures, tail periods and the
  "introduced" ambiguity — including Rejigg's own "reasonable" 6-month/named-list benchmark versus
  its "predatory" 12-24-month/no-list example), what a seller may and may not do during an exclusive
  engagement, how to end or renegotiate an engagement, and how an owner keeps improving the business
  during an exclusive without breaching it. 32 claim ids [KB-VSALE-048]–[KB-VSALE-079].

## Reading order

**Software track:**
1. Deciding whether/how to sell, or valuing a company → `selling-a-small-software-company.md`.
2. A real buyer is at the LOI stage or diligence has started → `deal-structure-and-diligence.md`.

**Local service business track:**
3. Valuing the business, understanding SBA buyer-financing mechanics, or preparing for diligence →
   `selling-a-local-service-business.md`.
4. Considering or already under an exclusive broker listing → `business-brokers-and-exclusives.md`.

All four files cross-reference each other by section number (`§N`) throughout — follow those links
rather than re-deriving the same guidance in two places. The software track and the local-service
track use disjoint claim-id ranges (`[KB-VEXIT-NNN]` for the software track, `[KB-VSALE-NNN]` for the
local-service track) so the two can be audited independently.

## Scope note

All four files are company-agnostic per this KB's writing rules — no specific company names, no
personal names, no absolute paths to any operator's own deal folders. Company-specific deal facts
(an actual company's own numbers, buyer conversations, or live negotiation state) live in that
company's own venture/deal folder, read separately by `capital-and-exit-strategist` per its agent
definition — never in this KB.
