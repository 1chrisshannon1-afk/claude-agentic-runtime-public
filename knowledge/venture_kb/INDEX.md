# Venture KB — index

Knowledge base for the venture department (`venture-director`, `capital-and-exit-strategist`,
`bizdev-outbound-strategist`, `partner-recruiter`): turning a company into money. Company-agnostic —
company facts live in each company's deal folder. Every claim follows
`../marketing_kb/shared/citation-style.md` (provenance tag + retrieval date; UNVERIFIED where a
primary source could not be read). Legal and tax content is information, not advice: each file says
where an attorney, CPA or securities counsel decides.

| Folder | What it answers | Main reader |
|---|---|---|
| [`recruiting/`](recruiting/INDEX.md) | Finding and choosing co-founders and advisors; equity splits, vesting, 83(b), advisor equity, IP assignment, worker classification | partner-recruiter, capital-and-exit-strategist |
| [`exit/`](exit/INDEX.md) | Selling a small software company (buyers, valuation, process, deal structure, diligence) and selling a local service business (SDE multiples, SBA financing, license/insurance transfer, brokers, exclusives and tails) | capital-and-exit-strategist, venture-director |
| [`capital/`](capital/INDEX.md) | Angel rounds and SAFEs (dilution math, entity type) and the securities rules a founder must follow (506(b)/(c), general solicitation, accredited investors, Form D) | capital-and-exit-strategist |
| [`outbound/`](outbound/INDEX.md) | Compliant B2B cold email and deliverability; the campaign method (levers, segmentation, list layering, replies) and rejected and bounded tactics; founder-led relationships, design partners, referral partners | bizdev-outbound-strategist |

Related shared doctrine (not repeated here): `../marketing_kb/compliance/` (CAN-SPAM, TCPA),
`../marketing_kb/sales/` (follow-up, CRM hygiene, trial-to-paid).
