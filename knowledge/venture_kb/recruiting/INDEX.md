# Recruiting — index

Knowledge base for the venture department's founding-team recruiting work. Read by
`venture-director`, `capital-and-exit-strategist`, `bizdev-outbound-strategist`, and
`partner-recruiter`.

- **[cofounder-search.md](cofounder-search.md)** — where to actually find an experienced technical
  or business/sales co-founder (YC Co-Founder Matching, Wellfound, founder communities,
  operator/angel networks, acquirer alumni, local ecosystem), how to run a first call and a trial
  project, scorecards and decision rules, back-channel reference checks, red flags, realistic
  search timelines, and how to pitch an already-built product honestly.
- **[equity-and-agreements.md](equity-and-agreements.md)** — co-founder equity splits for a
  late-joining co-founder (sourced frameworks and data), vesting and cliffs, single- vs.
  double-trigger acceleration, the 83(b) election and its 30-day deadline, advisor equity (the FAST
  agreement), stipends vs. salary, sale-proceeds-vs-equity inside a multi-business holding
  structure, IP assignment (CIIAA), contractor-vs-employee classification risk, and what must be in
  writing and when versus what must never be promised before counsel is involved.

Both files are information, not legal or tax advice — they name where an attorney, CPA, or
securities counsel must decide, and never instruct signing or promising anything.
