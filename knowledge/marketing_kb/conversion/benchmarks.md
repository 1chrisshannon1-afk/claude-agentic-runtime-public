# Conversion Benchmarks

Added 2026-09-19. Every figure here is a **reference point** for judging whether a measured
company number warrants investigation — never a target, and never a substitute for a company's own
measurement. A benchmark quoted without its source and retrieval date is exactly the failure this
corpus's house style exists to prevent; re-verify rather than remember.

## Landing-page conversion rate

- **All-industry median: 6.6%** across ~41,000 landing pages / 464M pageviews / 57M conversions,
  Q4 2024 data (third-party, Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report, via clickminded.com, retrieved
  2026-09-19).
- **Demo-request pages: 3.8% median / 6.1% top quartile / 9.3% top decile** (third-party, CorePPC
  2026 via clickminded.com, retrieved 2026-09-19 — this is a **different source from the Unbounce
  figure above**; do not merge the two or attribute the demo-page figures to Unbounce).
- **Financial-services paid search: 10.1% median** (third-party, Unbounce, retrieved 2026-09-19).

## Trial shape (already settled elsewhere in this corpus — restated here only as a pointer)

Card-required/opt-out trials convert 35-55% trial-to-paid (median ~44%) vs. no-card/opt-in — see
`forms-and-signup-friction.md`'s "## Trial/signup shape — what the evidence says about card-required
vs. not" section for the full citation. Do not restate a divergent number here.

## Dropped as unverified in this pass

A B2B-SaaS 2-5% / 8-15% conversion band and a "~3% paid search for B2B SaaS" figure were both
considered and dropped — neither source was opened/verified in the 2026-09-19 pass, and a
benchmarks file is precisely where an unverified number does the most damage, since it will be
quoted as settled. Do not add either back without opening and verifying the source first.
