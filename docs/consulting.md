# Consulting

This repo is free and open source (MIT). Free support is GitHub issues, best effort, with no
response-time guarantee; see [`SUPPORT.md`](../SUPPORT.md). Having it installed and tuned for your
team is a paid engagement, not a support request. This page is the full offer.

## Who it is for

Teams whose Claude Code rollout has stalled: agents overwriting each other, nothing reviewed before
it lands, no record of what ran or what it cost.

## The two engagements

| | Audit | Setup sprint |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | Your current setup reviewed, a gap analysis, and a roadmap | The guardrails installed and tuned to your stack, and your team trained |
| Length | 3 to 5 days | 1 to 3 weeks |
| Starting price | $1,500 to $2,500 | $7,500 to $15,000 |

The prices are starting ranges, not fixed quotes.

Live debugging of your own production incident is part of the paid setup sprint. It is not something
the free issue tracker can offer.
